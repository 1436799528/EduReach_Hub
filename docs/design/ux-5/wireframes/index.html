EduReach UX-5 wireframes

Structural wireframes for the student and administrator applications. Greyscale regions describe hierarchy and placement; they are deliberately not screenshots of the current build.

StudentHomepage — decide where to start

A first-time visitor identifies the platform and starts a practice paper in one tap.

 StudentStudent dashboard — what to do next

A signed-in student sees their next action, their numbers, and recent activity without reading three empty states.

 StudentMy CBT — performance in plain language

A student reads their average, trend and weakest subject, then starts the recommended paper.

 StudentCBT setup — choose paper with confidence

A student understands the rules, picks subjects, sees time and question count, and starts without surprises.

 StudentExamination — calm, single-focus paper

A student answers, navigates and submits without losing time or state.

 StudentResults — understand the outcome

A student knows their score, what it means, and the next useful step.

 StudentProfile — edit with context

A student sees completeness, edits grouped information, saves, and sees the saved state.

 StudentNotifications — status you can act on

A student scans unread updates and opens the related request or result.

 AdminAdmin overview — platform state at a glance

An administrator sees workload, risk and recent activity in one screen.

 AdminAdmin navigation — grouped by job

An administrator finds a section by the job they are doing, not by database table.

 AdminUsers — find, inspect, act

An administrator finds a student, understands their state, and takes a safe action.

 AdminQuestion management — review safely

An administrator filters questions, inspects one, and edits with provenance visible.

 AdminExamination management — publish with confidence

An administrator sees bank health, subject counts and publication state before publishing.

 AdminService requests — work the queue

An administrator sees requests needing action, opens one, and changes status with a reason.

 AdminContent — publish and verify

An administrator sees what is live, what is scheduled, and what needs review.

 AdminSystem settings — explain consequences

An administrator changes a setting and understands its blast radius.
