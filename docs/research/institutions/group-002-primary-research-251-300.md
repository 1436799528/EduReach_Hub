# Institution Intelligence — Primary Research Batch 251–300

**Research date:** 17 November 2026  
**Inventory source:** `data/institutions/institution-inventory-329.json` on `data/institution-intelligence-foundation`  
**Scope:** Records 251–300, in inventory order. This is an evidence-led research pass, not a claim that all 50 institutions have complete admission profiles. Missing current-session evidence is explicitly marked.  
**Admission session priority:** 2026/2027. Session dates and deadlines below are treated as session-specific, not reusable defaults.

## Research controls

- NUC recognition/listing establishes institutional identity/status; it does **not** establish approval or accreditation of every programme.
- A general UTME minimum is not a programme-specific cutoff. Use the university's current course-level notice and JAMB IBASS subject combination before publishing eligibility.
- Do not treat third-party admission adverts, user-generated listings, or an active portal alone as proof of NUC approval or programme accreditation.
- Where an official 2026/2027 notice was not found in this pass, the record remains **unverified for current admission details** rather than inheriting old cutoffs.
- The NUC Nigerian University System (NUS) directory was checked as the regulator identity reference. It lists Phoenix University, Plateau State University, Prime University, Prince Abubakar Audu University, Regnum Medical University, Unique Open University and other institutions in this range. [NUC NUS directory](https://enuc.nuc.edu.ng/nus)
