## Cross-record data-quality findings

1. **Canonical names/aliases:** Record 314 → Delta State University of Science and Technology (DSUST); record 323 → West Midlands Open University; record 328 → Yusuf Maitama Sule Federal University of Education; record 313 → University of Port Harcourt. Preserve original labels as aliases.
2. **Potential identity verification queue:** University of Maritime Studies, Oron (309), University of Offa (312) and Zamfara State University (329) need direct regulator and official-domain evidence before activating admission profiles.
3. **Do not mix score types:** UI cutoffs are aggregate points; UNILAG publishes course merit/catchment aggregates; YSU and UNIJOS publish programme-specific UTME cutoffs. Store these as distinct measures.
4. **Professional programmes:** Institutional recognition does not establish MBBS, Nursing, Pharmacy, MLS, Radiography or Law programme approval/accreditation. Keep programme-level evidence separate.
5. **2026/2027 evidence:** Strong current official evidence captured for UI, UNILESA, UNILORIN, UNIJOS, UNILAG, UNN, UNIPORT, UNIUYO, University on the Niger, Veritas, Wellspring, Westland, Wigwe and YSU. Other records remain explicitly incomplete.
6. **Production safety:** Documentation-only change; no production Supabase records or migrations were modified.
