## Evidence rules used
- NUC accreditation is programme-specific. A university-wide statement such as “all courses accredited” is not sufficient evidence for every individual programme. NUC describes its statutory role as accrediting degree programmes and publishing programme accreditation results: https://www.nuc.edu.ng/project/accreditation/
- The NUC accreditation technical-report index provides institution/programme/status entries and, in individual reports, visitation dates and Commission decisions: https://www.nuc.edu.ng/accreditation-tech-report-view/
- JAMB's official IBASS Eligibility Checker is the authoritative dynamic check for institution + programme + entry mode + O'Level + UTME subject eligibility: https://eligibility.jamb.gov.ng/. English Language is preselected. A general university admission page is not a substitute for the programme-specific JAMB check.
- UTME cut-offs and admission requirements are session-specific. Do not carry forward a prior session's thresholds.
