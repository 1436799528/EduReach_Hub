# Group 002 — Primary institutional research: 151–200

**Destination:** Draft research branch only. No production Supabase records were read or modified.

## Method and evidence rules

1. The inventory's `schoolName` and `state` fields define the ordered records; they are not proof of NUC recognition or programme availability.
2. NUC's Nigerian University System directory is the primary identity/recognition cross-check. An official university website is used for current admissions, programmes, portals, leadership, and institutional claims.
3. A university-wide admission notice is not evidence that every programme is approved or open. Programme-level accreditation, professional approvals, exact JAMB IBASS combinations, and session-specific cut-offs require programme-level evidence.
4. Dates and cut-offs below are session-specific where explicitly published. Closed windows remain labelled closed; no current availability is inferred from an old notice.
5. Where reliable evidence was not found in this pass, the record is marked **pending** rather than populated with guessed values. This is a research register, not permission to publish unsupported admissions data.
