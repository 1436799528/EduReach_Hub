# Group 001 — Secondary verification, batch 061–070

Research date: 2026-11-17
Branch: data/institution-intelligence-foundation
Scope: regulator evidence, admission and programme catalogue checks for institutions 61–70.
Important: only explicitly surfaced NUC programme decisions are marked verified. Institutional statements and historical notices remain separate from current regulator evidence.

## Verification standard
- NUC states that it coordinates programme accreditation, maintains the programme status record and publishes programme accreditation results: https://www.nuc.edu.ng/project/accreditation/
- The NUC accreditation technical-report index and individual decision pages are used as programme-level evidence: https://www.nuc.edu.ng/accreditation-tech-report-view/
- JAMB's dynamic IBASS Eligibility Checker is the official programme-specific route for checking institution, course, entry mode, O'Level and UTME subject eligibility: https://eligibility.jamb.gov.ng/
- Do not mark a programme accredited because the university website calls it accredited. Do not treat old admission cut-offs as current.

## JAMB subject-combination checks
- Use JAMB IBASS for each exact course/institution pair: https://eligibility.jamb.gov.ng/
- The checker requires selecting entry mode, institution type/category, institution, programme, O'Level subjects and UTME subjects. English Language is preselected.
- No course combination in this batch has been marked JAMB-verified from a general university page alone.
