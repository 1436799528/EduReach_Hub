## Research rules

1. The existing EduReach inventory order is preserved. This batch is not re-sorted to match the NUC directory's geographic presentation.
2. NUC's Nigerian University System (NUS) directory is the registry baseline for institutional name, ownership, state and year established. The current directory reports 328 universities (77 federal, 69 state, 182 private); the EduReach institution inventory has 329 rows. Reconcile the 329th record and aliases separately; do not delete a record based on the count difference alone.
3. NUC listing or an enabling Act is not evidence that every advertised programme has been resource-verified or accredited. Programme-level accreditation must be checked against the exact NUC report, programme name, campus and decision date.
4. University-published course combinations and cut-offs are provisional source material. JAMB IBASS must be checked for each institution, programme and entry mode before EduReach marks a combination as verified.
5. A 2026/2027 admission window that has already closed must not be shown as open. General UTME thresholds are not departmental cut-offs.
6. Status labels below describe the evidence captured in this research pass, not a judgement on institutional legitimacy.
