## Research rules

1. The NUC Nigerian University System directory is the primary institutional identity and licence-status reference. A listing establishes the institution's presence in the directory; it does **not** prove that every advertised programme is approved or currently admitting.
2. Admission notices are session-specific. A 2026/2027 notice is not reused as evidence for another session.
3. Programme lists and entry requirements are captured only where surfaced from official institutional pages or official admission documents. Third-party directory listings are supplementary, not a substitute for NUC programme approval or JAMB IBASS checks.
4. Where this pass did not retrieve sufficient primary evidence, the item is marked **pending** rather than populated by inference.
5. UTME cutoffs, O'Level combinations, subject combinations, deadlines and programme accreditation require programme-level verification before student-facing use.

## Cross-record data-quality issues

1. **Inventory naming reconciliation:** Mewar University should be compared against the NUCIS/NUC canonical styling “Mewar International University”. The NUC NUS register should be the source of truth for final legal names.
2. **Potential false/ambiguous identities:** National University of Science and Technology Abuja, Northwest University Kano, Ojaja University, Omega University Kaduna and Pearl University Calabar require direct NUC confirmation. Keep these records quarantined from student-facing admission recommendations until resolved.
3. **Distinct Northwest institutions:** Do not merge Northwest University, Kano with NorthWest University Sokoto or a historical Northwest University name without documentary proof.
4. **Special admission workflows:** NOUN and NDA have distinct admission processes. Do not apply a generic Post-UTME template to all their pathways.
5. **Programme-level verification:** Medical, nursing, pharmacy, optometry, radiography and other regulated programmes require programme-specific approval/accreditation checks. A university's general NUC listing is insufficient.
6. **Session timing:** Some retrieved admission windows have elapsed (MAU Yola and OAU). Current status must be checked again before displaying “open”.
7. **Search quality:** Some current directory sources are useful for identity discovery but not adequate for regulatory approval or admission status. Keep them labelled secondary.
