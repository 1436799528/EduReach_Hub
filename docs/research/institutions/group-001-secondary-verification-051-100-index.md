## Coverage
- Institutions 51–100 have received a secondary evidence pass: NUC programme-report search, current official admission/programme-page checks where located, and explicit JAMB eligibility-source review.
- This is not a full JAMB validation of every programme. The official IBASS checker is dynamic and requires institution/programme/entry-mode selection; no blanket combinations have been marked verified.
- NUC FULL/INTERIM status is recorded only for programmes with individual or clearly surfaced NUC report evidence. A result for one course does not apply to the entire university.
- Current cut-offs are recorded only when attached to a specific session and official source. Historical thresholds remain historical.
- Institutional name/alias, operational status, course-list and programme-duration conflicts remain flagged.
- Institutions with missing direct sources are not treated as having no programmes; their evidence status is marked incomplete.
