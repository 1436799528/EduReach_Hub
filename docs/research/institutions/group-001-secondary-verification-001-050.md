# Group 001 — Secondary verification, institutions 001–050

Research date: 2026-11-17  
Branch: `data/institution-intelligence-foundation`  
Scope: second-pass verification of current institutional identity, 2026/2027 admissions evidence, programme catalogue evidence, NUC programme-level accreditation evidence, and JAMB eligibility source requirements.

## Evidence rules

- NUC's current Nigerian University System directory is the registry baseline for identity, ownership, location and year established. It currently reports 328 universities: 77 federal, 69 state and 182 private. The EduReach inventory contains 329 rows; the difference requires a record-level reconciliation and must not be resolved by deleting a presumed duplicate.
- NUC accreditation is programme-specific. A FULL or INTERIM result applies only to the named programme and report/decision, not every course at that university.
- The NUC technical-report index can surface repeated or conflicting records. Where the same programme appears with more than one status, preserve the conflict until report dates and Commission decisions are reconciled.
- JAMB IBASS is the authoritative dynamic check for institution + programme + entry mode + O'Level + UTME subject eligibility. No blanket subject combinations are marked verified in this batch.
- UTME cut-offs and admission availability are session-specific. A historical threshold must not be displayed as a 2026/2027 cut-off.
- Institutional admission pages and programme catalogues are university claims; they do not replace NUC accreditation or JAMB eligibility checks.
